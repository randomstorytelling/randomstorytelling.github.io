The pitch

Summary

Why now. Creatine is one of the most studied ingredients in nutrition science, and for decades it lived in the gym. It is leaving the gym now. The people asking us about it are busy parents, people on calls all day, and women over 40 hearing about creatine for the first time. We built the gummy for them: four gummies, the full 5 g the research uses, and a flavor you look forward to.

Why we will succeed. The product already sells, in three flavors, on vybrancelabs.co, Amazon, TikTok Shop and Target.com, and to stores at a 50% margin. 621 clinicians share the Watermelon on FrontrowMD without being paid to. This round is not a bet on a new idea. It is more of the one that works.

How you grow with us. Back the round and you own a piece of the next run. The terms are set with Wefunder and shown in the box on the right. Every investor gets the first bags off the line, and the perks climb from there.

The product. Creatine Monohydrate Gummies. 5 g creatine, 500 mg taurine and 120 mcg vitamin B12 in every serving of four. Vegan, sugar-free, gluten-free, non-GMO, no artificial dyes. Watermelon, Citrus Mango and Green Apple, colored naturally.

The Opportunity

In 2017 a concussion sent me down a road I did not choose. Light hurt. Noise hurt. Thinking slowed. Hardest of all, I could not move, and movement is medicine. Without it, mind and body drain each other.

Looking for anything that might help me recover, I found creatine. Athletes had trusted it for decades. I saw how many more people it could serve. That belief became Vybrance Labs, founded in Austin with Martin Crowley.

Here is the opportunity in one line: creatine works for far more people than have ever taken it, and most of them will never scoop a powder. A gummy that tastes good, carries the full dose and looks like something you keep on the counter is how they start.

Adventure Hike, CityWalk, Floor Work. Austin, every month.

Our Product

One product, done right. Four gummies carry 5 g of creatine monohydrate, plus taurine and vitamin B12. The dose the research uses is the dose in the bag. A bag is 120 gummies, 30 servings, one month.

Taste is our north star. If it is not good enough to look forward to, it does not ship. Three flavors: Watermelon, Citrus Mango and Green Apple. Made in a cGMP facility in the USA. Third-party tested, every lot.

Watermelon is the one people call out first. 621 clinicians share it on FrontrowMD. When a national TV shopping buyer tasted all three this summer, Watermelon was the one she named. This round makes more of it.

Why We'll Succeed

It already sells, in four places. vybrancelabs.co, Amazon, TikTok Shop and Target.com. Stores buy it at $25 a bag, sell it at $49.99, 12 to a case, Net 30. That is a 50% margin for the retailer, the number buyers ask for first.

The proof is independent. FrontrowMD clinicians can receive samples and are never paid to write an evaluation. 621 of them share Watermelon. Two of their full reviews are below.

It is a community, not a customer list. Adventure Hike, CityWalk and Floor Work run in Austin every month, in person, and A Day of Vybrance is our yearly activation. The films on this page came out of those days.

The team has done the hard part. Martin leads the formulation, from the creatine dose to the taste. I lead the brand, the films and the design through VYB LABS. Josh Walker designed the packaging you are looking at, and Moyo Oyelola directs the films.

Vybrance means what it sounds like. We would rather have a thousand people who take four gummies a day and show up to a hike than one investor who never opens the bag.
