The Opportunity

In 2017 a concussion sent me down a road I did not choose. Light hurt. Noise hurt. Thinking slowed. Hardest of all, I could not move, and movement is medicine. Without it, mind and body drain each other.

Looking for anything that might help me recover, I found creatine. Athletes had trusted it for decades. I saw how many more people it could serve. That belief became Vybrance Labs, founded in Austin with Martin Crowley.

Here is the opportunity in one line: creatine works for far more people than have ever taken it, and most of them will never scoop a powder. A gummy that tastes good, carries the full dose and looks like something you keep on the counter is how they start.

Adventure Hike, City Walk, Floor Work, A Day of Vybrance.

Watermelon is the one people call out first. 621 clinicians share it on FrontrowMD. When a national TV shopping buyer tasted all three this summer, Watermelon was the one she named. This round makes more of it.
