The pitch

We make creatine gummies. Four gummies a day, five grams of creatine, and the taste comes first. Three flavors are on the shelf today. Watermelon is the one 621 clinicians share on FrontrowMD, so Watermelon is the one we are making more of.

This round pays for one 5,000 bag run of Watermelon, the freight to bring it in, and the launch. That is it. No new flavor, no new category. More of the thing that already works. The goal is $70,000, and most of it is the run itself.

We are a design led science company in Austin. Every lot is third-party tested. The gummies are vegan, sugar-free, gluten-free and non-GMO. They sell on vybrancelabs.co, Amazon, TikTok Shop and Target.com, and to stores at $25 a bag, $49.99 on the shelf, 12 to a case.

Invest and you get the first bags off the line. Thank you for reading.
