What it is

What you are buying

The card. Gross sales, discounts, ad spend, contribution margin. Yesterday next to the last seven and the last ninety. One question: did it do more than yesterday? Green light, put more there. Red light, pull back and fix before you spend another dollar. That is the whole read.

The listing loop. We start with a recorded brand DNA hour. I sit with you and ask a fixed set of questions about what your brand is and is not. That hour becomes the standard your listing is read against, title, bullets, images, backend keywords, alongside what the best sellers in your category do. The loop suggests. You decide. It never edits a live listing.

The halo read. Social sends people to Amazon. The card names which creator or post moved your branded searches and your units, so you know who to send more product to. No guessing from a screenshot.

The plumbing check. Traffic with no sales usually means a leak. A flavor out of stock, a listing pulled over an expiration flag, a price swing Amazon did not like, an order the fulfillment bridge rejected. The check reads the pipes every morning and names the leak before it costs a week.

The chief of staff. A person, not a chatbot. Expert in Amazon and in your business. For your first 30 days after install they read the card with you, tune the system to your business, and carry the work that needs hands. The glue. Nothing here acts on its own. It reads, it suggests, a human decides.
