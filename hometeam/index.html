Questions people ask

Founder run brands

How do I keep an eye on my Shopify and Amazon store overnight?

Home Team runs thirteen stations against your own accounts while you sleep and leaves one short list each morning. It reads your store, your money, your inventory and your inbox, so nothing depends on you remembering to check a dashboard.

Is there a service that watches my ecommerce business for me?

Yes. Home Team is an overnight watch built for founder run Shopify and Amazon brands. It is read only on every account: it will surface a problem and draft the reply, but it never sends, never buys and never moves money.

How is this different from an ecommerce operations platform?

A platform is a place you log into. Home Team sits above your stack and reads it, including whatever platform you already pay for, so the value in those tools reaches you without you opening them. It does not replace anything you run.

What does it cost to have my store monitored?

Home Team is $4,000 to install and $1,500 per month after that. The install connects your accounts and tunes the thirteen stations to your business. The monthly keeps the watch running and re-tunes the stations as the business changes.

Can AI run my ecommerce operations without making things up?

That is the design constraint here. Every claim Home Team makes carries the artifact it came from, and when it cannot verify something it says so by name instead of guessing. A station that finds nothing still reports, because silence is treated as a fault rather than as good news.

What access does it need to my accounts?

Read access to your store, your marketplace seller account, your bank and your operations inbox. Nothing more. It is read only and draft only by design, which is what makes handing over that access reasonable.
