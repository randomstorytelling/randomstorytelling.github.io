FAQ

Custom denim

How much does a custom embroidered denim jacket cost?

Every jacket starts at $750 all in with three patches, and that includes sourcing and buying the vintage jacket. Six patches is $1,050. Ten patches is $1,450. Each patch after the third is $100. A $250 deposit starts the work and is credited to the piece.

How do I get a one of one jacket made?

It starts with a free fifteen minute call about the story you want on it. Then a $250 deposit opens the design, we source the vintage jacket, and you approve a drawing before anything is stitched.

Do I have to supply my own jacket?

No. We find and buy the vintage jacket, and that is already inside the price. If you have a specific piece you want used, bring it up on the call.

Why is it priced by the number of patches?

Because the patches are the story. A jacket carrying three moments is a different piece of work from one carrying ten, so the price follows the work rather than a flat guess.

What does the $250 deposit cover?

The design: the conversation about your story, the placement, and the drawing you approve before any stitching. It is credited toward the finished piece, so it is not an extra fee on top.

Who makes the jackets?

They are made with Crane's Eye View, a collaboration out of Austin. Random Storytelling handles the story and the design, and the embroidery is done by hand.
