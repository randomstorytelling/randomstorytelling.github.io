RANDOM STORYTELLING started in 2017 with stories embroidered on denim jackets, one random moment tied to the next. Lawrence Whitaker III founded it as a creative platform. It grew into Vybrance Labs™, VYB Labs, its production arm for corporate wellness, events and film, consulting for CPG brands, websites built to be found in Google and AI answers, acting, and Base Camp, a calendar for Texas film and TV. Today RANDOM STORYTELLING is the practice behind everything he makes and sells, and what we built for our own brand, we build for yours. Everything he creates. The pieces, the rooms and the reps carry the same idea: humor, health and creativity.
