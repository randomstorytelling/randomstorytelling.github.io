What Austin film events are coming up?

Base Camp tracks 330 upcoming rooms across Texas film and TV: classes, screenings, showcases, mixers and festival submission deadlines. That count is as of September 29, 2026. The full list is on the calendar feed, free to subscribe to at https://randomstorytelling.com/basecamp.ics.

Is there an Austin film meetup?

Yes, several. The recurring rooms on Base Camp include Austin Film Meet, The Hideout Theatre, ColdTowne Theater, Austin Film Festival and Women Writing for Film and Television ATX. Base Camp puts their dates on one calendar. Show up to the same room twice. That is how crews form.

How do I meet people in the Austin film industry?

Pick two or three rooms and go back to them. Base Camp makes that easy: one calendar of the mixers, classes, screenings and showcases in Texas film and TV. One calendar, not a dozen sites and mailing lists.

Is there a calendar of Austin film and TV events I can subscribe to?

What is Base Camp?

Base Camp is a free calendar and a free monthly email for creatives and crew in Texas film and TV. It gathers the classes, screenings, showcases, mixers and festival deadlines into one place. It is published by RANDOM STORYTELLING, founded by Lawrence Whitaker III, and it is rooted in Austin.

Is Base Camp free?

Yes. The calendar feed is free and the monthly email is free. Subscribe to the calendar at https://randomstorytelling.com/basecamp.ics and to the email at https://randomstorytelling.beehiiv.com/subscribe.
