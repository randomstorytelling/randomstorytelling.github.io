FAQ

What film and TV events are happening in Austin right now?

Base Camp tracks 47 upcoming rooms across Texas film and TV, including classes, screenings, showcases, mixers and festival submission deadlines. The full list is on this page and in a calendar feed you can subscribe to at https://randomstorytelling.com/basecamp.ics.

Where do filmmakers and actors meet in Austin?

The recurring rooms on Base Camp include Austin Film Meet, Austin Film Society, The Hideout Theatre, ColdTowne Theater, Austin Film Festival and Women Writing for Film and Television ATX. Base Camp puts their dates on one calendar so you can show up to the same room twice, which is how crews actually form.

How do I meet people in the Austin film industry?

Pick two or three rooms and go back to them. Base Camp exists so that is easy: one calendar of the mixers, classes, screenings and showcases in Texas film and TV, instead of a dozen separate sites and mailing lists.

Where can I take film or acting classes in Austin?

Base Camp currently lists 13 upcoming classes and workshops, from Hideout Annex, Austin Film Society, Carol Hickey Acting Studio and The Actor’s School. Dates and links are on this page.

Are there upcoming Texas film festival deadlines?

Yes. Base Camp is tracking 12 upcoming submission deadlines for Texas and Texas-adjacent festivals, listed alongside the events so a deadline does not sneak past you.

Is there a calendar of Austin film and TV events I can subscribe to?

Yes, and it is free. Subscribe to https://randomstorytelling.com/basecamp.ics with the Apple or Google buttons on this page, or paste that address into any calendar app. It updates as the list updates. There is also a free monthly email.
