What you are buying

A trainer gets an hour with a player and then loses sight of them. The player goes off and shoots alone, with no eye on the form and no way to tell which miss is the one worth fixing.

Swish is that eye, on the player’s own phone. It films a shot, reads the shooting form from body pose, scores nine measured mechanics against ideal ranges, and names the single highest leverage fix rather than a list of everything wrong.

A trainer seat is a superset of the player app. It films the trainer’s own reps, and it adds a roster plus a seven day log for every player: shots, days, average, change versus last week, best, and the fix showing up most.

The nine measured mechanics are leg load, elbow set, elbow flare, set point height, release arc, follow through, balance, stance width and drift. The feedback arrives in one of five coaching voices: The Shot Doctor, The Skills Trainer, The Hype, The Professor and The Fundamentalist. They are archetypes and composites. No real coach, athlete, brand or trademarked system is named anywhere in the app, deliberately.

FAQ

Trainers and players

How do I track my shooting form?

Film a shot with Swish on your own phone. It reads your shooting form from your body pose, scores nine measured mechanics against ideal ranges, and names the single highest leverage fix instead of a list of everything wrong. The nine are leg load, elbow set, elbow flare, set point height, release arc, follow through, balance, stance width and drift.

Is there an app that analyses a basketball shot?

Swish does. It films a shot, reads the form from body pose, scores it and names one fix, in one of five coaching voices. It runs entirely on the player’s own device, so the video never leaves the phone and only numbers are stored.

How do coaches track players between sessions?

The player sends their week as a readable report plus a paste-able payload. The coach pastes it and it merges into that player’s row. The roster carries a seven day log per player: shots, days, average, change versus last week, best, and the fix showing up most. Numbers only, no video, and there is no server.

Does it upload my video?

No. Swish runs entirely on the player’s own device. The video never leaves the phone, only numbers are stored, and there is nowhere for a clip to be uploaded to because there is no server.

What does Swish cost?

Solo is free and covers your own reps plus three players. Roster is $29 per month for up to twenty five players. Program is $79 per month for unlimited players and coaches. There is a fourteen day trial of the full roster.

Do my players have to pay?

No. Swish is sold to trainers and players use it free. A trainer seat is a superset of the player app: it films the trainer’s own reps and it adds a roster plus a week log for every player.
